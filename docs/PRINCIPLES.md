# Creative Principles

## Purpose

Build a reusable library of short audio loops before considering any visual use.

## Principles

1. Sound supports attention without demanding it.
2. Favor ambience, intimacy, restraint, and space.
3. Presence matters more than conventional sound design.
4. Beds establish a continuous world.
5. Gestures create nearby, tactile events.
6. Repetition should feel intentional; loop seams should disappear.
7. Generate combinations a person would be unlikely to choose but likely to appreciate.
8. Randomness must be constrained and reproducible.
9. Software expands the creator's possibility space; it does not replace taste.
10. Automate technical work. Reserve approval for human listening.

## Quality test

An approved track should be:

- technically valid;
- loop-safe;
- restrained rather than empty;
- distinctive without becoming distracting;
- emotionally open enough to support more than one interpretation;
- worth hearing repeatedly.
